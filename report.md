## Why realised savings moved, 2026-09 to 2026-10

- Total movement: $246,291
- Volume, more or less work actioned: $171,652
- Rate, conversion of actioned into landed: $74,639 (87% to 89%)

Splitting it this way separates a sales question from a product question. Volume moves when customers approve more work. Rate moves when the actions stop converting, and that is the one worth a standup.
